Privacy Policy

Huemi: Widgets & Home Themes · updated October 3, 2026

Short version: your look is built on your iPhone, and Huemi never reads your photos.

Photos

When you apply a vibe or save an icon set, Huemi asks for add-only access to Photos and adds the wallpaper and icon pictures there. Huemi cannot see, read or upload anything in your photo library.

Widgets

Huemi widgets read one setting — which vibe you applied — from storage shared only between the Huemi app and its widgets on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

  • Your three onboarding answers (mood, colours, what you want on screen).
  • Which vibe you applied and which pictures you saved.

Huemi does not use the camera, microphone, location or contacts. It has no accounts.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. To manage subscriptions and measure how the paywall performs, Huemi uses Adapty as a processor. Adapty receives an anonymous app-generated profile ID, purchase and subscription status, device model, OS version, app version, country/region derived from the IP address, which onboarding screen was reached and the onboarding answers. Huemi does not collect your name, email or advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Huemi is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting data

Delete the app to remove everything it stored on the device; pictures you saved to Photos stay there until you delete them. For data held by Adapty for your anonymous profile, write to netkaktakto@gmail.com with the profile ID from the feedback email.
