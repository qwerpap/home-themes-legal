How Huemi treats your data

A theme maker has no business reading your photos, and Huemi does not.

Photos: add only

When you save a wallpaper or icon pictures, Huemi asks for add-only access to Photos. It can put pictures in, and cannot see, read or upload anything already there.

Widgets

The Vibe Clock, Calendar and Art widgets read one value, the vibe you applied, from storage shared only between the app and its widgets on your iPhone.

Kept on the phone

  • Your three quiz answers: mood, colours, what you want on screen.
  • The vibe you applied.
  • Which pictures you saved.

No camera, microphone, location, contacts or accounts.

Shortcuts

Icon sets are placed with Apple’s Shortcuts app, which runs on your device under Apple’s rules. Huemi hands it pictures; it does not receive information back about your apps.

Purchases and Adapty

Apple handles payment. Adapty (adapty.io) is our subscription processor and receives a random profile ID, purchase status, device model, OS and app version, country or region inferred from the IP address, the onboarding screen reached and your quiz answers. No name, email or advertising ID, and no tracking across other companies’ apps or sites.

Children

Not for under-13s; we do not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting

Delete the app to remove what it stored; pictures you saved stay in Photos until you delete them. For Adapty data, write to netkaktakto@gmail.com with your profile ID.
