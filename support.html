Common questions

  • Setting the wallpaper: open the picture Huemi saved in Photos, tap Share › Use as Wallpaper.
  • Adding widgets: touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, find Huemi.
  • Icons: in Shortcuts add “Open App”, then Share › Add to Home Screen and choose the Huemi picture.
  • Restore Pro: Huemi › My Screen › Settings › Restore purchases.
