Huemi support

Quick fixes for the usual snags.

Wallpaper did not change

iOS needs you to set it: open Photos, pick the saved wallpaper, Share › Use as Wallpaper.

Icons

Follow the guide inside the app. Shortcuts opens the app with a short banner; the original icon stays in your App Library.

Widgets look old

Re-apply the vibe; the widgets follow. If they still look stale, remove and re-add them.

Subscription

Restore in Huemi Settings. Cancel in iPhone Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.
