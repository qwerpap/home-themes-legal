What Huemi does — and doesn't

Huemi offers twelve themed looks. Applying a look switches Huemi's own widgets at once and saves the wallpaper and icon pictures to Photos. iOS does not let apps set your wallpaper or replace other apps' icons: you set the wallpaper from Photos, and you place icon pictures with Apple's Shortcuts app, which creates a shortcut that opens the app. The original app icons stay in your App Library.
